07 / FAQ

Common questions about scope and delivery

Do GEO and SEO need to run together?

Not necessarily. They share website and content foundations but have different objectives. Work can be phased around your current priorities.

Can you guarantee rankings or AI recommendations?

No fixed ranking, citation or AI recommendation is guaranteed. We evaluate work against agreed scope, verifiable deliverables and progress data.

Do we need a new website?

Not always. We first assess access, indexing, structure and enquiry paths. Improve the existing site where possible and discuss rebuilding or migration only when needed.

How are timing and fees determined?

They depend on site size, target markets and workload. Deliverables, responsibilities and reporting are agreed in advance. Media budgets are separate from service fees.

How do you measure visibility in AI answers?

We use a fixed set of relevant questions, selected platforms and observation dates, then review mentions, cited sources and factual accuracy. AI answers can vary and monitoring is directional rather than a guarantee.

Can you support multiple languages and markets?

Yes, scope is planned market by market. We review search intent, local terminology, hreflang, content quality and conversion journeys before deciding which languages to prioritise.
